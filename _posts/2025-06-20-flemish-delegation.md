Our project **"Transforming Engineering Education with Multimodal GenAI and Intelligent Agents for Improving Students' Problem-Solving Skills"** has been selected as one of four Digital Futures projects to present to a **Flemish delegation from Belgium** on **June 25th**.

## About the Visit

The delegation of approximately 50 participants includes politicians, representatives from governmental bodies, companies, and researchers from the Flemish region of Belgium. Digital Futures has chosen our project alongside three other initiatives to showcase the cutting-edge research happening at the intersection of AI, education, and technology. This presentation is an exciting opportunity to share our research with international stakeholders and explore potential collaborations between Swedish and Belgian institutions.
