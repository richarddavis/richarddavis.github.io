### 1. Google Search Console Setup (15 minutes)
- [ ] Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
- [ ] Add your site: `https://richarddavis.github.io` (or your custom domain)
- [ ] Choose "URL prefix" method
- [ ] Download the HTML verification file and upload to your site root, OR
- [ ] Add the meta tag to your `_config.yml`:

- [ ] Click "Verify"
