## Computational Research Expertise

My research at KTH Royal Institute of Technology involves extensive use of advanced computational methods and data science techniques to understand and enhance learning processes. This technical expertise spans multiple domains relevant to both academic research and industry applications.

### Machine Learning & AI

- **Deep Learning Frameworks**: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras for educational data analysis and model development
- **Neural Network Architectures**: CNNs for visual learning analysis, RNNs/LSTMs for sequential data modeling, Transformers for educational NLP applications
- **LLM Engineering**: Fine-tuning and deployment of large language models for educational applications, including prompt engineering and model optimization
- **Generative AI**: Development of AI tools for creative learning experiences, automated content generation, and personalized educational materials
- **Computer Vision**: Implementation of vision systems for analyzing learning behaviors in maker spaces and hands-on learning environments

- **Quantitative Research Methods**: Advanced experimental design, statistical modeling, multivariate analysis, and causal inference techniques
